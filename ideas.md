# AIIA Clinical Portal Design Brief

## Direction
Institutional Clinical Green: a serious, readable healthcare-research system for AIIA. Warm white surfaces, charcoal typography, deep muted institutional green, subtle borders and restrained shadows. No gradients, glow, glassmorphism, neon, purple, futuristic styling or generic AI SaaS patterns.

## Principles
- Clarity before decoration.
- Tables and timelines are first-class clinical work surfaces.
- High contrast and compact density for projector and ward-round use.
- Status colors are semantic and sparse.
- AI is a quiet workflow assistant, never the visual hero.

## Typography and layout
Use a strong serif display face for page titles and a neutral sans-serif for controls and data. Desktop uses a stable left navigation rail and utility top bar; mobile collapses navigation into a sheet. Prefer 12-column grids, compact table rows, 16–24px spacing and modest 8–12px corner radii.

## Brand identity
Use the provided official AIIA logo asset without recreating, recoloring, distorting or redesigning it. Pair it with the institutional name and the portal title in restrained black/charcoal text.
